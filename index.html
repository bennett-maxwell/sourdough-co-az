The Brand

Sourdough & Co. (World of Sourdough Franchising, LLC) is a quick-service sandwich restaurant franchise offering handcrafted sandwiches, soups, salads, pasta, beverages and catering from eat-in/carry-out retail locations. FDD Item 19 reports average 2024 sales of $719,491 across 64 locations open all year (range $297,320-$1,117,988); Item 20 shows 80 outlets systemwide as of 2024-12-31 with zero terminations in 2022-2024. Initial investment $285,800-$568,725; 5% royalty; 2-mile protected (non-exclusive) territory.
